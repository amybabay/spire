# Scenario 3:
- Start with 2 control centers + 1 data center
- In anticipation of the hurricane, we replace one vulnerable control center with a preemptively deployed mobile control center
- After hurricane passes and both original CCs are operational, we go back to the original configuration

---

1. System initialized with 3 sites: 2 Control Centers (CC1, CC2) and 1 Data Center (DC). Each site runs 6 replicas.

2. In anticipation of a hurricane, Control Center 2 is replaced with a preemptively deployed Mobile Control Center (MCC).

3. After the hurricane passes, both original control centers are operational.

4. System reconfigured: returned to original configuration with CC1, CC2, and DC.
